# Playbook PB-EXP-01 — Low-Touch Digital Expansion

> **In plain English.** How to grow hundreds of small accounts you can't call individually. The engine finds the ones already outgrowing their plan — more people active than seats bought, users bumping into features they don't have — and sends one honest, evidence-based nudge, never more than two in two weeks, and never to an account with an open problem. Bigger opportunities get handed to a person. §7 (what a nudge is allowed to say) is the part worth reading even if you never run the playbook.

**Pillar:** Expansion · **Priority:** P3 (P2 when a `now` play is ≥ 20% of current ARR) · **Cooldown:** 30 days · **Owner:** Digital programs (CSM pooled); sales-assist above threshold · **Prompt chain:** `expansion.whitespace_expansion_finder` (+ `adoption.feature_depth_audit` as a sub-step when feature-gate hits are the trigger)

This playbook finds expansion that is already happening in the product and turns it into contracted ARR without a human touch until one is warranted. It runs across the SMB and digital segments — hundreds or thousands of accounts per CSM — so every step is automated by default and a human enters only when the indicative ARR crosses the sales-assist threshold or the customer replies.

The discipline that keeps it from becoming spam: **no play without usage evidence**, **no more than two asks per account per 14 days**, and **no expansion on an account with any red flag**. The engine is as careful about *not* firing as it is about firing.

---

## 1. Trigger conditions

Any one of the following, for `account.segment ∈ {smb, digital}` and `health.band != "red"` (`src/orchestrator.py::trig_exp_01`):

| # | Condition | Customer 360 path | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Seat utilization ≥ 85% | `usage.seat_utilization ≥ 0.85` | Approaching or past the license ceiling; over-deployment (> 1.0) is the strongest form |
| T2 | Feature-gate hits ≥ 3 in 14 days | `sum(usage.feature_gate_hits_14d[].hits) ≥ 3` | Users are trying to use something their tier does not include |
| T3 | PQL score ≥ 70 | `usage.pql_score ≥ 70` | Product-qualified lead model (owned by Growth) says ready |
| T4 | DAU/MAU ≥ 0.35 | `usage.dau_mau_30d ≥ 0.35` | Habitual daily use in a segment where the median is 0.22–0.28 |

**Readiness gate (evaluated by the prompt, enforced by the engine):** the play is deferred with a `deferral` action if any of: open P1/P2, `payment_days_past_due > 0`, `contraction_flag_90d`, champion departed, CSAT < 3.5, or an `urgency ≥ high` negative sentiment signal in 30 days. A deferred account is re-evaluated after cooldown.

**Suppression:** open PB-EXP-01 action in 30 days → `signal_update`. Any P0 playbook firing in the same run suppresses this one.

## 2. Segmentation and motion

| Indicative ARR (annualized list) | Motion | Human involvement |
|---|---|---|
| < sales-assist threshold (`pricing_catalog.sales_assist_threshold_arr[segment]`; default digital $12k, SMB $20k) | **Self-serve**: in-product upgrade prompt + one automated email, both generated by the prompt and routed to the digital platform | None unless the customer replies or clicks "talk to someone" |
| ≥ threshold | **Sales-assist**: same digital nudges + `sales_handoff` record to the pooled CSM/AE queue | One 20-minute discovery call within 15 business days |
| Any play with `play_type ∈ {business_unit_expansion, geo_expansion}` | Always sales-assist | Human qualifies the sponsor |

Digital nudges are `actions[]` of type `digital_nudge` and are the only engine outputs that execute without human approval — and only when `channel == "in_app"` and the segment is `digital`. Email nudges in SMB require approval of the template once per quarter, not per send (CS Ops approves the template; the engine fills the variables).

## 4. Timeline (self-serve path)

```
Day 0   trigger fires → whitespace_expansion_finder emits readiness + ≤ 4 plays + nudge copy
        → in-app nudge queued for the admin persona (digital only) · email draft queued (SMB: template-approved)
        → account.expansion_candidate = true

Day 1   in-app nudge shown on next admin login (max 1 per 14 days per account)

Day 3   email nudge sent if no in-app interaction (send_after_days from the action)

Day 10  if upgraded: outcome `expanded`; cooldown
        if clicked but not upgraded: sales-assist handoff regardless of threshold (intent signal)
        if no interaction: nothing more this cycle; re-evaluate after 30-day cooldown with fresh evidence

Day 30  cooldown ends; trigger re-evaluated. A second cycle may use the second-ranked play, never the same copy.
```

## 5. Timeline (sales-assist path)

```
Day 0   as above, plus sales_handoff record → #cs-expansion-queue with qualifying facts and do_not_say
Day 1–3 pooled CSM reviews brief; sends the sponsor communication (approval: one click)
Day ≤15 discovery call held; opportunity created by the human (never by the engine)
Day 30  checkpoint: opp open → PB-EXP-01 closed `handoff_complete`; no reply → one automated follow-up, then cooldown
```

## 6. Claude execution scripts

### 6.1 Batch (the normal path)

The daily orchestrator run evaluates every account; nothing to invoke. To run the digital book only:

```bash
python -m src.orchestrator --input ./feeds/customer_360_digital.jsonl --playbook PB-EXP-01 --out ./out --concurrency 16
```

### 6.2 Single account, on demand

```bash
python -m src.orchestrator --input ./feeds/customer_360.jsonl --account-id acc_digital_8841 --playbook PB-EXP-01 --out ./out
```

Produces:

```
out/acc_digital_8841/PB-EXP-01.whitespace_expansion_finder.action.json
    expansion_brief.readiness            {ready, blocking_conditions[], evidence_paths[]}
    expansion_brief.plays[]              ≤ 4, each sized with sizing_basis and sizing_confidence
    expansion_brief.sales_handoff        {recommended, ae_notes, qualifying_facts[], do_not_say[]}
    actions[]                            digital_nudge (in-app copy), crm_write expansion_candidate, sales_handoff (if ≥ threshold)
out/acc_digital_8841/PB-EXP-01.comm_1.md   (SMB email nudge or sales-assist sponsor email; approval front-matter)
```

### 6.3 What the prompt is told (summary)

* Confirm readiness first; any red flag → deferral, not a play.
* Enumerate plays only from evidence: utilization, over-deployment, gate hits, stated intent in transcript/NPS signals, unowned modules with persona + outcome fit (from the feature audit).
* Size with the catalog formulas; mark everything `indicative`; never quote.
* Pick the motion by segment and threshold; for digital, emit the in-app copy as a `digital_nudge` action (headline ≤ 80 chars, body ≤ 300, one CTA).
* Write `do_not_say`: the sensitivities a human must not step on (a stale pricing comment, a recently closed ticket).
* Max two asks per 14 days; respect `actions_last_30d`.

## 7. Nudge copy standards (enforced by the prompt, reviewed quarterly by digital programs)

* **Evidence-led.** The nudge states the specific thing the customer did ("Your team has 23 people active on 20 seats", "4 people tried to set up SSO this week") — never generic ("Unlock more with Pro!").
* **One CTA.** "Add 5 seats" or "Start SSO setup" — not both.
* **No urgency theater.** No countdowns, no "offer ends", no scarcity. The urgency is theirs (they are hitting the ceiling), not ours.
* **Exit path.** Every in-app nudge has a "not now" that suppresses the play for 30 days and a "talk to someone" that routes to sales-assist regardless of threshold.
* **Tone.** Second person, present tense, under 50 words for in-app, under 120 for email.

Example (generated for a digital account with utilization 1.15 and gate hits on `sso_scim`):

> **Headline:** 23 people are sharing 20 seats
> **Body:** Three teammates have been added since September and your workspace is now over its seat limit. Adding 5 seats keeps everyone active, and your IT lead's SSO setup attempt from Tuesday will unlock with the Business tier.
> **CTA:** Add 5 seats · *Not now* · *Talk to someone*

## 8. Automated output artifacts

| Artifact | Where | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Expansion brief (`expansion_brief`) | action JSON | Internal; no approval |
| In-app nudge (`digital_nudge` action) | pushed to the digital platform queue via `ActionRouter` (live mode) | None for `digital` segment in-app; template-level approval for SMB email |

| Sales handoff record | `#cs-expansion-queue` message + `account.expansion_candidate = true` + `sales_handoff` action | Human accepts into the queue |
| Opportunity | **Never created by the engine.** `opportunity_suggestion` action with suggested name, amount band, and close window; the human creates it. | n/a |

---

## 9. Exit criteria

* Upgrade/expansion closed-won (any channel) → outcome `expanded`.
* Sales-assist opportunity created → outcome `handoff_complete` (tracked separately to closed-won in `action_outcomes`).
* Customer selects "not now" → outcome `declined`, 30-day suppression.
* No interaction after both nudges → outcome `no_change`, cooldown.
* Readiness gate failed → outcome `deferred` with the blocking condition; the account's retention playbooks take over.

## 10. Metrics

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nudge-to-upgrade conversion (self-serve) | ≥ 6% within 30 days | by play type; seat_expansion typically 2–3× tier_upgrade |
| Handoff-to-opportunity (sales-assist) | ≥ 40% within 15 business days | |
| Opportunity-to-closed-won | ≥ 25% within 120 days | |
| Expansion ARR per 1,000 accounts per quarter | baseline + 30% after two quarters | the number the digital programs manager owns |
| Nudge "not now" rate | ≤ 15% | above this, copy or thresholds are wrong |
| Deferral rate | 10–25% | below 10% the readiness gate is too loose; above 25% the trigger thresholds are too loose |
| Complaints / unsubscribe attributable to nudges | 0 per 10,000 sends | hard stop; any instance pauses the playbook for review |

## 11. Worked example

**Account:** Brightline Studio · digital · ARR $9,600 (20 seats × $480) · health 82 green · utilization 1.15 (23 active on 20 seats) · `feature_gate_hits_14d`: `sso_scim` ×4 (gated to Business), `api_access` ×2 (gated to Business) · admin persona engaged 2 days ago · no tickets · NPS 9 · no contraction · 210 days to renewal.

**Trigger:** T1 (utilization ≥ 0.85, in fact over-deployed) and T2 (6 gate hits).

**Readiness:** ready; no blocking conditions; evidence `health.band`, `support.open_tickets` (empty), `contract.payment_days_past_due` (0).

**Plays:**
1. `seat_expansion`, now, likely — target seats = max(23 × 1.15, 20 × 1.25) = 26.5 → 30 (pack of 5) → +10 seats × $480 = $4,800 indicative (sizing_confidence high; basis shown).
2. `tier_upgrade` to Business, now, likely — (840 − 480) × 30 = $10,800 indicative; evidence gate hits on two Business-gated features; sponsor: the admin.
3. No module or BU play (no evidence).

**Total indicative** $15,600; weighted $9,360. Above the $12k digital sales-assist threshold → sales handoff recommended, `ae_notes`: "Over-deployed by 3 seats and actively hitting SSO/API gates; admin engaged this week; NPS 9. Lead with the SSO setup they already tried. do_not_say: none." Actions: `digital_nudge` (in-app, copy as in §7), `crm_write` `expansion_candidate = true`, `sales_handoff` (approval), `opportunity_suggestion` "Brightline — Business tier + 10 seats, $14–16k, close within 45 days". One sponsor email draft to the admin (approval). Confidence 0.86.

**What happened (illustrative, not a customer result):** The admin clicked "Talk to someone" on the in-app nudge on day 1. Pooled CSM held a 20-minute call on day 4; upgraded to Business with 30 seats on day 9 at $25,200 ARR (the customer chose 30 seats over the suggested 26). Outcome `expanded`; NRR contribution +$15,600.
